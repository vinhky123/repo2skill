This log preserves every `Ruling:` line from the P0 local-generator progress ledger in its original chronological order. Each ruling retains its original reason and cost text. These are implementation contract decisions, not a replacement for the canonical design and engineering contracts. The canonical contracts govern exact APIs; later rulings and contract updates supersede earlier wording where they differ (for example, the export recovery guard names). Append future rulings before final release.
